Invio Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Invio keeps your invoices, clients, receipts and settings on your phone. The developer does not run a server that receives them, and Invio has no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

This policy explains what the Invio app (“Invio”, “the app”) does with information. “We” means the developer of Invio.

What stays on your phone

Everything you enter is saved in the app's private storage on your device:

  • Invoices, estimates, payments and recurring schedules
  • Your clients' names, phone numbers, email addresses and addresses
  • Your business details, payment details and invoice settings
  • A logo and a signature image, if you choose them
  • Receipts you add: the vendor, amount, date, category, note and photo
  • Saved items and your settings

We do not receive, see or store this information. It is not uploaded to us.

What Invio does not do

  • It does not ask you to create an account.
  • It does not show ads.
  • It does not use analytics or advertising trackers.
  • It does not sell or share your information for marketing.
  • It does not use your microphone, location or contacts.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Notifications. To remind you about due invoices and recurring invoices. These reminders are created on your phone and shown on your phone only. You can turn them off in the app or in your phone's settings.
  • Camera. Only if you choose to photograph a receipt. The photo is saved inside the app on your phone and is not uploaded to us.
  • Photos. When you choose a logo, a signature or a receipt photo from your gallery, Invio opens your phone's photo picker. The app only receives the image you select.

Information that leaves your phone only when you choose

  • Sharing a PDF. When you share an invoice, your phone's share menu opens and you choose where it goes. What the other app does with it is covered by that app's own policy.
  • WhatsApp. If you tap “Send via WhatsApp”, Invio opens WhatsApp with a message ready to send. The message goes through WhatsApp only when you send it, and WhatsApp's policy applies.
  • Email and SMS. If you tap Email or SMS, Invio opens your email or messaging app with a message ready. For email, the invoice PDF is attached. The message is sent only when you press send in that app, and that app's policy applies.
  • Payment links. If you add a payment link, it is printed on your invoices as text and a QR code. Invio does not process payments.
  • Backups and exports. A backup file or CSV export contains your invoice, client and receipt details. A backup does not include receipt photos, which stay on the phone. You decide where to save these files. They are not encrypted, so keep them somewhere safe.

Subscriptions

Invio offers an optional paid plan. Payments are handled by Google Play. We do not see or store your card details.

To confirm which plan you own, Invio uses a subscription service called RevenueCat. It receives your purchase information, an anonymous identifier for the app installation, and basic technical details such as your device type and IP address. We use this only to give you the features you paid for. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Android device backup

If you have Android's built-in backup turned on, your phone or Google may include the app's data in your device backup. That is controlled by your phone's settings and Google's policies, not by us.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your phone until you delete it. You can delete individual invoices, clients, receipts (with their photos) and saved items inside the app. Uninstalling Invio removes its data from your phone. Backup files and exports you saved yourself stay where you put them, and you can delete them there.

Children

Invio is a tool for people who run a business or do paid work. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and the date at the top.
